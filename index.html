Frequently asked questions

How do I know if the commission I am charged exceeds my contract?

Your settlement shows the rate applied to each order; your contract shows the negotiated rate. The difference, multiplied by volume, is what you can claim. Line-by-line verification is our job, not yours.

What if the platform refuses?

You keep the file: the settlement lines and the gap calculation. The contracted rate is enforceable, and an internal audit request with itemized evidence succeeds far more often than an oral complaint.

Is my data safe?

You send us your settlements by email, the analysis is done by a person, then the files are deleted from our inboxes after the report is delivered. No connection to your accounts, no bank statements kept.

Which countries does it work in?

Everywhere these platforms operate: DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, Postmates across the US. The formats differ, the checks are the same.
